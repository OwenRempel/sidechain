Why it sounds good: overtones

Every note is secretly a stack of higher frequencies (overtones). When two notes share overtones they lock together; when their overtones land close but not equal they rub and "beat". That's consonance vs tension, and it's physics, not rules.

Which notes are safe?

Pick a chord, then tap notes over it. Chord notes land and feel solid. Key notes lean, and want to step to a chord note. Outside notes clash, so use them only in passing. The same note can be solid over one chord and leaning over the next.
